Hi, I'm Kris. I'm a front-end developer at Lemone. I care about HTML that means something, CSS that holds up, and websites that work for everyone (including people who use a keyboard or a screen reader).

## How did I get here?

I started out in interaction design. My old portfolio dates from 2016 and shows more wireframes than code. Somewhere along the way I found out I liked building the thing more than drawing it.

## What will you find here?

Front-end patterns I use and reuse, posts about accessibility, and the small decisions that make a website better. This site is also my playground. New CSS gets tried here first, before it ends up in client work.

## Why Mostly Harmless?

That is how the Hitchhiker's Guide describes Earth. It felt like a fair summary of a personal website. Don't panic.
